You do not need an account to play. These terms cover what you agree to if you make one.

1. What this is

Arcade Campus Hub is a hobby project — a catalogue of browser games with optional accounts, friends and messaging. It is run by one person, not a company. There is no support contract, no uptime promise, and it may change or disappear.

2. Your account

  • You are responsible for what happens under your account. Don't share your password.
  • There is no password reset. No email is collected, so a forgotten password cannot be recovered — only the account deleted. Pick something you'll remember.
  • One person, one account. Don't make accounts to get around a suspension.
  • Don't impersonate someone else, including staff.

3. How to behave

Messages and profiles reach real people, many of them classmates. Don't:

  • harass, bully, threaten or target anyone
  • post sexual content involving minors — this is reported, not just removed
  • share someone's personal information
  • spam, scam, or try to phish passwords
  • attack the site or other users' accounts

Staff can remove messages, suspend accounts and delete them. Serious things get passed on to whoever needs to know.

4. The games

The games are third-party works owned by their creators, collected here and served as-is. They are not made by this site, they may break, and they may be removed. If you own a game listed here and want it taken down, send a message through feedback and it will be.

Whether you're allowed to play games on the network you're using is between you and whoever runs that network. The compatibility flags describe how reliably something loads — not permission to load it.

5. Your content

Messages, images and profile text stay yours. By posting them you allow the site to store and show them to the people you sent them to. Staff can read reported content for moderation. Deleting your account removes them.

6. No warranty

This is provided as-is with no guarantee it works, keeps your data, or stays online. Back up anything you'd mind losing — Settings → Export does that in one click.

7. Ending it

You can delete your account at any time from Settings, and it's immediate. Accounts may be suspended or removed for breaking section 3.

8. Changes

These terms have a version, recorded against your account when you agree to them. If they change materially you'll be asked again rather than assumed to have agreed.
